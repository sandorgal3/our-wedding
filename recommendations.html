Make A Trip Of It

Recommendations

Traveling in for the wedding? Here are a few of our favorite picks — places to stay near the venue in Neszmély, restaurants worth seeking out, and things to do in Budapest and beyond if you're making a trip of it.

All of these are within about ten minutes of Hilltop Borbirtok, in the villages that make up the Neszmély wine region along the Danube.

Dunaszentmiklós · Apartments

Duna Residence

A holiday park of modern apartments (sleeping 4–6) right in the heart of the wine region — great for families or groups.

Dunaszentmiklós · Guesthouse

Rózsa Panzió

A cozy guesthouse with its own wine cellar for tastings, plus a sauna and fitness room to unwind after the party.

Dunaszentmiklós · Guesthouse

Bike Stop Duna

Right on the Danube Bike Trail with a large private garden — a relaxed option if anyone's arriving by bike.

Süttő · Guesthouse

Duci Udvar II Vendégház

A highly-rated riverside guesthouse just 150m from the Danube, in the quiet village of Süttő.

Dunaalmás · Apartments

Anemona Vendégháza

A small apartment-style guesthouse in Dunaalmás, a good option for groups traveling together.

Neszmély · Winery Guesthouse

Rábai Katalin Borászat és Vendégház

A working winery right in Neszmély itself, with a hot tub and a shared kitchen and lounge — about as close to the venue as it gets.

Tata · Boutique Hotel

Malom & Kacsa Boutique Hotel

For a more traditional hotel stay, this boutique property in nearby Tata has a fitness center, garden, and terrace — Tata's lakeside old town is worth an afternoon on its own.

Fine Dining

Stand25 Bib Gourmand

Modern Hungarian cooking rooted in tradition, from a chef duo trained at the country's top tables.

Fine Dining

Borkonyha Winekitchen

A Michelin-starred spot pairing refined Hungarian dishes with an excellent local wine list.

Classic Hungarian

Rosenstein Restaurant

Old-school Hungarian-Jewish comfort food — goose liver, stuffed cabbage — done with real care.

Casual · Local Favorite

Kőleves Vendéglő

A cozy, buzzing bistro in the Jewish Quarter serving hearty Hungarian classics.

Israeli · Small Plates

Mazel Tov

A stunning courtyard restaurant with Middle Eastern small plates — lively and photogenic at night.

Café · Historic

New York Café

One of the most opulent cafés in the world — worth a coffee and pastry just to see the room.

The venue itself is about an hour from Budapest, so if you're building a longer trip around the wedding, here's what's worth seeing in the city on your non-wedding days.

Landmark

Hungarian Parliament Building

Stunning by day and lit beautifully at night — book a tour or simply admire it from the river.

Views

Fisherman's Bastion & Buda Castle

Fairy-tale terraces on the Buda side with the best panoramic views of the city and the Danube.

Thermal Bath

Széchenyi Thermal Bath

A grand, canary-yellow bathhouse with outdoor thermal pools — a true Budapest institution.

Bridge

Chain Bridge

The iconic bridge linking Buda and Pest — beautiful to walk across at golden hour.

Nightlife

Szimpla Kert Ruin Bar

The original ruin bar — a maze of mismatched rooms in a crumbling courtyard building.

Market

Great Market Hall

A beautiful 19th-century market hall for paprika, souvenirs, and Hungarian street food.

Green Space

Margaret Island

A car-free island in the middle of the Danube — perfect for a quiet walk or bike ride.

Memorial

Shoes On The Danube Bank

A quiet, moving memorial along the riverbank — worth a few minutes of reflection.

Day Trip · 25 min

Szentendre

A picturesque riverside artist town with cobbled streets, galleries, and pastel houses.

Day Trip · 2 hrs

Eger

A baroque town known for its castle, thermal baths, and the "Valley of the Beautiful Women" wine cellars.

Weekend Trip · 2 hrs

Lake Balaton

Central Europe's largest lake — vineyards, beach towns, and the volcanic hills of the Balaton Uplands.

Day Trip · 2.5 hrs

Tokaj Wine Region

Rolling vineyards famous for sweet Tokaji wine — cellar tours and tastings available.
